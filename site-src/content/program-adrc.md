## What it does

The ADRC is the front door when you do not know which door you need. Staff give information and referrals, help with benefits applications, and coordinate transitions from a facility back into the community. If your situation does not fit neatly into any single program, start here.

## Who it serves

- People 60 and older.
- Adults 18 to 59 with disabilities. This is also the door for riders under 60 to qualify for [SRDA's vans](../transportation/).

2-1-1 coverage extends across eleven southeastern Colorado counties.
