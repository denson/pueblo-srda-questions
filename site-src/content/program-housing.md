## What it is

97 units across two complexes, Richmond Plaza and Union Plaza, adjacent to the Joseph H. Edwards Active Adult Center downtown. The housing is a collaboration between SRDA, the City of Pueblo, and the Pueblo Housing Authority, for people who need supportive services close at hand.

Living next door to the senior center means the meals, classes, and services on this site are a short walk away rather than a van ride.

### Subsidized housing through the Pueblo Housing Authority

**Pueblo Housing Authority**, 201 S Victoria, (719) 542-6741. For people 62 and older. The Authority keeps the waitlist for these seven buildings, and the list must be open to add your name. Apply at hapueblo.org; you will need income verification, a copy of your birth certificate and Social Security card, and a driver's license or state ID.

## Interpretation cautions

- Rentals run through the Pueblo Housing Authority, not SRDA's front desk.
  Send rental questions to (719) 542-6741.
- Nothing in the public record states income limits or subsidy structure
  for Richmond and Union Plaza specifically. The handout's categories
  (Housing Authority subsidized, one-third-income buildings, max-rent,
  unsubsidized) are the structure to use.
- When helping someone: the actionable advice from the visit is to watch
  hapueblo.org and the Housing Authority's social media for waitlist
  openings, be ready with the four documents, and act the day a list opens.
  Expect roughly a year on a list; do not promise faster.
