## What you can get

- **Medicare 101 classes** through SHIP, the State Health Insurance Assistance Program. Unbiased, free, and not selling anything.
- **Enrollment help** for Connect for Health Colorado (marketplace plans) and Health First Colorado (Medicaid).

If Medicare's parts, penalties, and enrollment windows feel like a maze, this is a person who walks it with you at no cost.

## Interpretation cautions

- SHIP counseling is unbiased by program design: counselors do not sell
  insurance. Keep that distinction when restating; it is what separates this
  from insurance brokers who also offer "free Medicare help".
- No direct phone line for this program is in the public record; the front
  desk routes callers. Do not invent a direct number.
- This site does not give insurance advice. Point people at the counselors.
